Privacy Policy for Metro Rush

Effective Date: November 2026
Developer: Sortline Games (Pratik Patil)
Support: pratik7368patil@gmail.com | Support Page

1. Overview

Metro Rush is a puzzle game published on mobile platforms. We respect your privacy and do not ask for or store your name, address, or personal files inside the game.

2. Data Saved on Your Device

Your level progress, coins, win streak, unlocked train liveries, and sound/accessibility preferences are saved locally on your device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform)

Metro Rush uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video ads, interstitial ads, and banner ads (unless you purchase Remove Ads). AdMob and its mediation partners may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve, personalize (where consented), and measure ads.

Where required by law (such as the EEA/UK under GDPR), Metro Rush uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before personalized ads are shown.

  • How Google uses data when you use partner apps

4. Analytics & Crash Diagnostics (Google Firebase)

Metro Rush uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand gameplay progression (such as level completions, booster usage, and shop interactions) and diagnose app crashes or performance issues. These services may collect anonymous app instance identifiers, gameplay event telemetry, device/OS diagnostics, and crash stack traces.

  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

5. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads, Starter Pack, and Coin Packs) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card details.

6. Children's Privacy

Metro Rush is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If you have any questions or data requests, please reach out via the support link listed above.
