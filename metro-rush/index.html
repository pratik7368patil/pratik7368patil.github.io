Metro Rush: Train Sort Puzzle

Clear the rush-hour crowd without jamming the platform! Tap commuters with a clear path to board the matching train coach, manage the 5-seat waiting bench, and shunt waiting coaches to the front before you run out of moves.

Game Highlights

  • Tap & Sort: Match commuters by line colour across busy 6×7 station concourses.
  • Tactical Shunting: Bring waiting coaches to the front within your shunt budget.
  • Stations & Twists: Travel through Sunny Station, Downtown, and Snowy Evening with Mystery commuters, Turnstile Gates, and 6-seat Express Coaches.
  • Accessibility & Offline Play: Built-in colourblind number badges, sound/haptics toggles, and full offline playability.
